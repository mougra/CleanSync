When you work from home, the desk is in view all day. Every mug, every cable
and every scrap of paper catches your eye at the moment you need to concentrate — which is why
order matters here more than in a room kept for guests. And it rests on three zones.

## The desk: an empty field first

Once a week, clear the worktop completely: everything comes off onto the floor or the bed.
Then put back only what you need for work:

- a laptop or monitor, keyboard, mouse, lamp;
- a stand or a tray for small items: pens, headphones, a USB stick;
- a mug — on a coaster or away from the keyboard, so you do not have to reach across it;
- papers — in one folder labelled "to sort out", not in a pile at the edge.

The rule is simple: only what you need today lives on the desk. Everything else goes in a drawer,
even if it "might come in useful". Check in a month: you will not take half of the drawer out.

## Cables

A tangle under the desk is not a small thing: dust collects in it, it catches on your feet and it breaks
right at the plug.

1. **Coil the slack into a loop** and fix it with a hook-and-loop tie. Rigid plastic
   cable ties are only good for things that no longer move.
2. **Label the tags.** A paper flag on tape saves you when you need to pull out one
   of six similar power supplies.
3. **Keep one extension lead with a switch.** Then at night all the equipment goes off,
   not one plug at a time.
4. **Chargers in one box.** Eight cables in a drawer turn into one tangle within a day.
5. **Do not wind the cable tightly around the block** — that is how the insulation breaks at the base.
6. **Vacuum under the desk** once a month: that is where most dust settles, and it gets
   into the equipment's vents.

## Equipment

- **Keyboard.** Turn it upside down and shake it, go between the rows with a soft brush,
  blow it through with a rubber bulb. With a damp cloth — only over the top and only slightly wrung out.
- **Screen.** A dry or slightly damp microfibre. Alcohol and window cleaner damage the coating.
- **Mouse and mat.** Soak the mat in soapy water for ten minutes, wipe the mouse with
  a damp cloth, and go over the wheel separately.
- **Laptop.** Once a month clean the vents with a soft brush: with a dusty heatsink
  it runs hot and loud.
- **Desktop tower.** Once every six months take off the side panel and blow the dust out on the balcony,
  not in the room.

## Light and posture

- **A warm lamp on the left,** if you are right-handed: the shadow does not fall on what you are writing.
- **The monitor at eye level.** A stand or a couple of books under it, so your neck is not tilted all day.
- **The chair once a month** should be gone over with a vacuum: crumbs collect between the armrests.
- **The floor under the desk** should be washed along with the rest of the room, not "sometime later".

## What is important to remember

Keeping a workspace clean does not take an hour. Five minutes at the end of the day is enough: put the pens
back in the tray, throw away the scraps of paper, close the laptop and take the mug away. In that time the desk does not have
time to silt up, and the morning begins without any digging around.
