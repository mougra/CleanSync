A dog in a flat means dirt from outside, hair on every surface and a smell that
appears unnoticed. The good news is that none of this needs a deep clean, just
three short habits.

## Paws after a walk

- **Keep a basin and a towel by the door.** Washing paws in the bathroom across the whole flat
  means carrying the dirt along the corridor.
- **Warm water without shampoo.** Shampoo every day dries out the pads; for daily
  washing water is enough, and for heavy dirt a mild soap once a week.
- **Dry between the toes and the pads.** That is exactly where moisture stays and causes
  irritation and smell.
- **In winter, washing is essential.** Road salt corrodes the skin and is then spread around the flat
  with melting snow.
- **In summer a damp towel is enough.** If it is dry and clean outside, a full basin is not needed.

## Hair and shedding

1. **Grooming is the main clean.** Go over short-haired dogs with a rubber mitt
   once a week, long-haired ones two or three times a week, and every day during shedding.
2. **Collect the hair before washing the floor.** A vacuum with a turbo brush or a wet rubber
   brush first, then the cloth: otherwise the hair turns into wet clumps in the corners.
3. **Keep a lint roller by the door.** It is quicker to take hair off your trousers in ten seconds
   than to wash it out afterwards.
4. **Cover the sofa with a throw.** A washable throw once a fortnight — and the sofa does not need
   cleaning all over.

## The bed, the bowls and the toys

- **The bed should have a removable cover.** Washing at 60 °C once a week removes both the hair
  and the smell, which is held in the fabric itself.
- **Wash the bowls every day.** A slimy film appears on the sides within a day, especially in hot weather.
- **Fabric toys go into the wash in a laundry bag**, and rubber balls should be soaked for ten minutes
  in soapy water and then rinsed.
- **Wash the doormat first of all.** It collects all the dirt from outside.

## Rugs, floors and textiles

- **The doormat is a consumable.** Wash it once a fortnight, not once a year.
- **Wash the floors every other day** in the hallway and the kitchen: that is where street dirt arrives.
- **Throws and sofa covers** should be washed at 60 °C once a fortnight, as should armchair covers.
- **Fabric rugs** should be vacuumed twice a week, and sent for a professional clean once a season.
- **Keep a separate cloth for the dog's places.** The smell soaks into the mop and is then spread
  through the whole flat.

## Smell: remove it, do not mask it

A freshener or an air spray only mixes with the dog smell — the result is worse. The smell
lives in four places: the bed, the rugs, the bowls and damp hair. Dry a wet dog
thoroughly, clean the rugs on a schedule, wash the bed, and air the flat for ten minutes
morning and evening.

## What is important to remember

Cleaning in a home with a dog rests on a daily ritual at the door and a weekly wash of
textiles. Fifteen minutes a day gives more than three hours on Saturday, after which
everything is back to how it was by the evening.
