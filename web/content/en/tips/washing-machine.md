A washing machine smells not because it is dirty, but because it is **damp and warm** —
perfect conditions for bacteria and mould. One short ritual a month is enough.

## What to do

1. **An empty cycle at 60 °C.** Pour 100–150 g of citric acid into the detergent drawer and
   run the longest programme with no laundry inside. The acid dissolves the scale and the smell.
2. **Wipe the seal.** Fold back the door gasket and wipe the inside with a cloth —
   that is where water and lint collect.
3. **Leave the door open.** After every wash — for an hour, so the drum can dry.

## Good to know

- Liquid detergent leaves less scale than powder if you wash often.
- Check the drain pump filter every six months: coins and lint have to be taken out by hand.
- Conditioner does not soften water — it does nothing for limescale, and in large doses it becomes
  a source of smell itself.

> If the smell remains after the acid cycle, the problem is the drain hose: water stands in the
> sagging section. The hose needs to be rehung or replaced.

## How long it takes

The cycle itself runs for about two hours, but your part is about five minutes. Track it as a monthly
habit and the washing machine will stay fresh.
