The main enemy of a quick clean is **running between zones**. Start at the door and move clockwise,
without turning back: that way you walk round the kitchen once and finish where you started.

## The route

1. **The worktop to the right of the hob.** Put the clutter back in its own places, wipe it down.
2. **The hob and the splashback.** Top first, then bottom: otherwise drips run down onto what is already clean.
3. **The sink.** Rinse it, wipe it dry — a dry sink looks clean all day.
4. **The worktop on the left.** The same as on the right: clear it, wipe it.
5. **The outside of the fridge and the handles.** The most noticeable fingerprints are here.
6. **The floor.** From the far corner towards the door, so you do not walk over what you have just washed.

## What helps you go faster

- **One cloth for surfaces, a second for the floor.** A third "just in case" only gets in the way.
- **An empty sink comes first.** While there are dishes in it, the feeling of mess does not go away.
- **A rubbish bag within reach.** Small debris does not need carrying across the whole kitchen.
- **A 30-minute timer.** Stop when it rings: an imperfect skirting board is not worth another half hour.

## What not to do

- Do not reorganise the cupboards during a quick clean: that is a separate job with a different mood.
- Do not start with the floor — you will have to wash it twice.
- Do not leave dishes to soak "for later" in a single sink: an hour on, it is already unpleasant.

> If the kitchen is always dirty, the problem is not the cleaning but the lack of a place for things. A bread bin,
> a tray for keys and a shelf for mugs solve half of the clutter.
