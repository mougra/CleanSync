Streaks on glass are almost always the result of two things: **cleaning in the sun** and **working
over dirt**. Fixing the order of steps is easier than choosing a product.

## The order of work

1. **Pick a cloudy day.** In the sun the water and the product dry faster than you can
   wipe them off.
2. **Frames and sills first**, then the glass: otherwise dust from the frames ends up on the glass again.
3. **Top to bottom.** Start in the top corner and work in a zigzag — drips will not cross
   the parts you have already cleaned.
4. **Take the solution off dry.** A squeegee or a microfibre cloth: do not let the water dry on its own.
5. **Buff it** with a dry microfibre cloth horizontally, and the water mark vertically: that way
   you can see where a mark is left.

## What to clean with

- Warm water with a drop of washing-up liquid is enough for ordinary dirt.
- Table vinegar (1:3 with water) lifts limescale well.
- Products with ammonia are not suitable for tinted glass or films.

## What matters outside

The outside is washed from the top down and only from stable footing. If the window is above the third
floor or the pane does not open, that is a job for industrial climbers, not for a cloth at home.

> Take the insect screen off and wash it separately: the dust from it flies straight back into the room.
