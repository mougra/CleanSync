The hallway is the busiest zone in the home: every day shoes, bags, coats,
keys, post and carrier bags pass through it. If a thing has no place, it stays where it was taken off.

## Five solutions

1. **Shoes for one season only.** The rest goes to the storeroom or the top cupboards:
   the hallway is not a warehouse.
2. **Hooks instead of hangers.** A coat on a hook takes a second, on a hanger five, so
   hangers get ignored.
3. **One tray for small items.** Keys, pass, wallet — always in the same place.
4. **A basket for post and bags.** Sort it once a week, not "when it piles up".
5. **A place for the bag.** A shelf or a hook at arm height: the bag stops falling on the floor.

## The order of sorting out

- Start with the floor: take away everything that is not meant to be there.
- Check the shoes: odd ones, worn out, out of season — out they go.
- Go through coat pockets: receipts, tickets, small change.
- Wipe the skirting boards and the door: street dust settles in the hallway.
- Put up a mirror if there is none: it visually widens a narrow space.

## What makes a hallway awkward

- A deep shoe rack without a tilt: shoes get stacked in two rows and the second one is never seen.
- A rail above head height: you cannot reach it, so things end up on a chair.
- No lighting: in the dark the mess is not noticed, but it still builds up.

> The one-minute rule: coming home, put away straight away whatever you have brought in. An hour later it is already
> "later", and by the evening it is "tomorrow".
